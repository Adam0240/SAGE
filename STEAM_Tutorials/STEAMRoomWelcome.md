# Welcome to the STEAM Room

## What is the STEAM Room?

The STEAM Room is a space in the Aiken Technical College Library where students, faculty, and staff can explore technology and work on creative projects. STEAM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering, Art, and Mathematics.

The room gives you a chance to try equipment you might not normally have access to, including 3D printers, laser engravers, and virtual reality headsets. You can get help with a class project, learn how something works, or talk through an idea you want to try.

You do not need to already know how to use the equipment. Helping you learn is part of what the room is here for.

## Who works in the STEAM Room?

The current STEAM Specialist is Adam Abbadusky.

Adam helps with equipment demonstrations, project planning, troubleshooting, and general questions about the technology in the room. He also maintains the equipment and creates instructions for using it.

If you have an idea but are unsure where to start, ask Adam. He can help you figure out what equipment or software would make sense for your project.

## What does the STEAM Room have?

### 3D Printing

The room has two Snapmaker A350 machines. One machine is setup as a designated 3D printer, and the other a designated laser engraver. A 3D printer builds a physical object from a digital model by adding material one layer at a time.

Projects can include models, keychains, educational materials, and custom parts. Adam can help you determine whether a model is suitable for printing and what preparation it needs.

### Laser Engraving

The Snapmaker machines also have laser engraving capabilities. Laser engraving can be used to add designs or text to compatible materials. What is possible depends on the material and the equipment being used.

Ask Adam about your material and design before starting a project.

### Virtual Reality

The room has virtual reality equipment, including a Meta Quest 3. VR lets you explore and interact with a digital environment using a headset and controllers.

### Arduino and Programming

Arduino kits are available for learning about programming and electronics. These can be used to explore how code interacts with physical components.

### Video Editing

The room also has access to Adobe video editing software. Adam can help with questions about editing and preparing video projects.

## STEAM Room Hours

The STEAM Room is open Monday through Thursday from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m.

## Who can use the STEAM Room

The STEAM Room is open to students, faculty, staff, and members of the public. You do not need to attend or work at Aiken Technical College to use the room.

Everything in the STEAM Room is free to use.

## What is SAGE?

SAGE stands for STEAM Artificial Guidance Expert. It is an application being developed to help people find information about the STEAM Room and its equipment.

The reference documents contain local instructions, equipment information, and troubleshooting notes. These documents can be updated when equipment changes or new information needs to be added.

The AI question-and-answer feature is still being developed. For now, you can open the reference documents directly to read the available information.

## Getting Started

If this is your first visit, start by asking about the equipment or explaining what you want to make. You do not need to arrive with a finished plan.

For instructions about a particular machine, open its reference document. If the instructions do not cover your question, ask Adam for help.
